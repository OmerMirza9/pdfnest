3. iLovePDF's strengths

iLovePDF has one of the broadest toolsets, a free tier that handles most casual needs, and reliable Office conversion. Its desktop and mobile apps are solid. If you want a single do-everything suite and don't mind uploading, it's a fine default.

4. Smallpdf's strengths

Smallpdf wins on polish — the interface is the cleanest of the three — and on e-signing and integrations (Google Drive, Dropbox). The catch is the stricter free-tier daily limit, which nudges you to a paid plan faster.
