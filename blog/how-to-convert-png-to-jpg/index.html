2. The transparency catch — and the fix

This trips people up: JPG has no transparency. If you convert a PNG with a see-through background straight to JPG, those transparent pixels have to become something — and a naive converter often turns them black. The fix is to fill transparent areas with a chosen background colour (white is usual) before encoding. PDFNest does this automatically and lets you pick the colour, so logos and graphics come out on a clean white background instead of an ugly black one.

4. Converting the other way (JPG to PNG)

Going from JPG to PNG won't add quality back — JPG already discarded detail — but it's useful when you need a lossless copy to edit, or a format that supports transparency you'll add later. Same tool: drop in the JPG and choose PNG as the output.
