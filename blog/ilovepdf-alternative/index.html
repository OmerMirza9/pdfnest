3. Where iLovePDF is still stronger

A fair comparison means being honest about the gaps. iLovePDF is a mature product, and there are things it does that PDFNest doesn't (yet):

  • Office conversions. PDF ↔ Word, Excel and PowerPoint need heavy server-side processing, which iLovePDF offers and PDFNest currently does not.
  • Dedicated apps. iLovePDF has desktop and mobile apps; PDFNest is web-only (though it works in any mobile browser).
  • Very large / batch jobs. Because PDFNest works on your device, extremely large files lean on your device's memory, whereas a server-based tool offloads that.

If those specific features are what you need, iLovePDF is a reasonable choice. For the everyday tasks most people actually do, PDFNest covers them privately and for free.

4. Is iLovePDF safe?

iLovePDF is an established service and states that it encrypts transfers and deletes files after processing. The honest nuance is simply where the work happens: with any upload-based tool, your file does leave your device and sit on a third-party server for a time. For most documents that's fine; for sensitive ones, a tool that never uploads at all removes the question entirely.
