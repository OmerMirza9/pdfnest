3. Where Smallpdf is still stronger

To be fair, Smallpdf is a mature product with features PDFNest doesn't have yet:

  • Office conversions (PDF ↔ Word, Excel, PowerPoint), which need server-side processing.
  • e-Signing and request-signature workflows.
  • Apps and integrations (desktop, mobile, Google Drive/Dropbox).

If your work depends on those, Smallpdf's paid plan is a reasonable choice. For the everyday tasks most people do, PDFNest handles them free and privately.

4. Is Smallpdf safe?

Smallpdf is an established company that encrypts transfers and says it deletes files after processing. The honest point is the same as with any online PDF service: your file does leave your device and sit on a third-party server for a while. For sensitive documents, a tool that never uploads removes that exposure entirely — which is PDFNest's whole design. See our guide on whether online PDF tools are safe.
