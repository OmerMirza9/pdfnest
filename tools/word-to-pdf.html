    Convert Word to PDF without uploading your document

    A PDF is the safest way to share a finished Word document: it looks identical on every device, can't be accidentally edited, and opens without Microsoft Office. PDFNest converts your .docx file entirely in your browser — it reads the document, rebuilds it as a clean web page, and hands it to your browser's built-in PDF engine. Because nothing is uploaded, even a confidential contract, CV or report never leaves your computer.

    What gets preserved

    Headings, paragraphs, bold and italic text, bulleted and numbered lists, tables and embedded images all carry over. Very advanced Word features — complex multi-column layouts, text boxes, headers/footers and footnotes — may be simplified, since they're rebuilt from the document's content rather than copied pixel-for-pixel. For everyday documents the result is clean and faithful.

    Need the other direction or a different format? Use HTML to PDF for web content, Markdown to PDF for notes, or Merge PDF to combine the result with other files.

    Word to PDF — FAQ

    How do I convert a Word document to PDF for free?

    Drop your .docx in above and click Convert to PDF. PDFNest reads it in your browser and opens a print dialog — choose “Save as PDF”. Nothing is uploaded and no watermark is added.

    Does it upload my document?

    No — the .docx is read and rendered entirely in your browser, so it never leaves your device.

    Will my formatting be kept?

    Headings, lists, tables, images and basic text styling are preserved. Very advanced layout features may be simplified.

    Do I need Microsoft Word or Office?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser — no Office, no account, no Adobe.

    Can I convert an old .doc file?

    This tool supports modern .docx files. Open an old .doc in Word and save it as .docx first.
