HTML to PDF — FAQ

Can I upload an .html file?

Yes. Use the "Upload .html file" button, or just paste your code into the editor.

Do my CSS styles work?

Inline and embedded styles work great. External stylesheets and remote images may not load due to browser security.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The PDF is generated entirely on your device.
