    Rotate PDF — FAQ

    Is the rotation saved permanently?

    Yes. The rotation is written into the PDF, so it stays correct in every app and on print.

    Can I rotate only some pages?

    Yes. Enter the page numbers in the “Apply to pages” box. Leave it empty to rotate all pages.

    Does rotating reduce quality?

    No. Rotation does not re-compress your pages, so quality is unchanged.
