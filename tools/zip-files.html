    Zip files and folders without uploading anything

    ZIP is the universal way to bundle many files into one and shrink them at the same time — for email attachments, uploads, backups and tidy hand-offs. PDFNest builds the archive entirely in your browser: files are read and compressed on your device, so even confidential documents never get uploaded.

    What compresses well — and what doesn't

    Compression works by finding repetition. Text-heavy files — documents, spreadsheets, CSVs, code, logs — often shrink by 50–90%. Already-compressed formats — JPG photos, MP4 video, MP3 audio and most PDFs — barely shrink at all, because their repetition has already been squeezed out. Zipping them still helps, though: you get one tidy file instead of twenty attachments. If the goal is smaller photos rather than one bundle, Compress Image is the sharper tool; for a bulky PDF it's Compress PDF.

    Create ZIP — FAQ

    How do I zip files for free?

    Drop your files or a folder in above, pick a compression level, and download the .zip. It's built in your browser — nothing uploaded.

    Can I zip a whole folder?

    Yes — drag the folder in (or use "Choose a folder") and its full structure, subfolders included, goes into the archive.

    How much smaller will files get?

    Text and documents often shrink 50–90%; photos, video and other compressed formats barely change.

    Are my files private?

    Yes — everything is read and compressed locally and never uploaded.
