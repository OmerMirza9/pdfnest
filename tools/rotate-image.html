    Rotate and flip photos without uploading anything

    Phones save photos sideways, scanners feed pages in upside-down, and front cameras mirror everything. Rotating fixes all of it — and PDFNest does it entirely in your browser: the image is turned on your device, so even private photos never get uploaded.

    Rotate or flip — which do you need?

    Rotate turns the image around its centre: 90° right for photos taken with the phone tilted left, 90° left for the opposite, 180° for upside-down. Flip mirrors it instead: horizontal flip fixes mirrored selfies and text that reads backwards; vertical flip fixes scans that went through the feeder face-down. You can combine both in one pass.

    Rotating by right angles is effectively lossless — the pixels are rearranged, not resampled — so your photo stays exactly as sharp as it was. Need to fix a sideways document instead? That's the Rotate PDF tool. Want to shrink the photo while you're at it? Follow up with Compress Image or Resize Image.

    Rotate Image — FAQ

    How do I rotate an image for free?

    Drop your JPG, PNG or WebP in above, pick 90° left, 90° right or 180°, and download. It's rotated in your browser — nothing uploaded, no watermark.

    Does rotating lose quality?

    No — right-angle rotation rearranges pixels without resampling. JPGs are re-encoded at high quality; PNG output is fully lossless.

    Can I rotate many images at once?

    Yes — add them all, apply the same rotation, and download everything as a ZIP.

    What's the difference between rotate and flip?

    Rotate turns the image; flip mirrors it. Horizontal flip fixes mirrored selfies, vertical flip fixes face-down scans.
