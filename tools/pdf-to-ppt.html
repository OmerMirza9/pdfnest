    Turn a PDF into editable PowerPoint slides

    Dropping a PDF into a presentation usually means awkward screenshots. PDFNest does it properly: it renders every page of your PDF into a high-resolution slide and packages them as a real .pptx file you can open in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides. Because every page is rendered locally in your browser, even a confidential report or deck never leaves your computer — no watermark, no account, no Adobe.

    How the conversion works

    In the default Editable text mode, PDFNest reads the real text out of each PDF page and rebuilds it as PowerPoint text boxes in their original positions, with the original font sizes — so you can click any line and edit it, restyle it or move it. Pictures and drawings inside the PDF aren't carried over in this mode, and text colours are simplified to black. When the exact look matters more than editing, switch to Exact snapshot mode: each page becomes a crisp image sized to its own slide, pixel-perfect, with your own text boxes and shapes added on top.

    PDF to PowerPoint — FAQ

    How do I convert a PDF to PowerPoint for free?

    Drop your PDF in above and click Convert to PowerPoint. PDFNest renders each page into a slide in your browser and gives you an editable .pptx — nothing is uploaded and no watermark is added.

    Is the text in the slides editable?

    Yes — the default Editable text mode turns every line into a real text box in its original position, ready to edit in PowerPoint. Prefer a pixel-perfect copy instead? Switch to Exact snapshot mode.

    Does it upload my PDF?

    No — the PDF is rendered entirely in your browser, so it never leaves your device.

    What file do I get?

    A standard .pptx that opens in PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides, with one slide per page.

    Do I need PowerPoint installed?

    No. The conversion runs in your browser; you only need PowerPoint, Keynote or Slides to open the result.
