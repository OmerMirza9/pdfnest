    Trim a PDF down to what matters

    Long PDFs often carry pages you don't need — blank pages, cover sheets, ads or duplicate scans. PDFNest lets you remove them in seconds by simply listing the page numbers. Your original is untouched; you download a fresh, cleaner copy. Everything happens in your browser, so your document stays private.

    Remove Pages — FAQ

    How do I choose which pages to delete?

    Enter page numbers separated by commas and use a dash for ranges, e.g. 2, 5-7, 10.

    Is my original changed?

    No. The tool creates a new PDF; your original file stays as it is.

    Want to keep only a few pages instead?

    Use the Split PDF tool to extract just the pages you want.
