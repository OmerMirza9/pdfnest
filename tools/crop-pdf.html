    Trim PDF margins without losing quality

    Cropping changes the visible area of each page. PDFNest sets a new crop box on the pages you choose, so the content is not re-rendered: text stays sharp and selectable, and images keep their full resolution. Margins are measured as the page looks on screen, so rotated pages crop the way you expect.

    Use Detect white margins to find where the content starts on the first page and trim the empty border around it — useful for scanned documents and slides exported with wide borders. Check the preview, adjust any side by hand, and apply the same crop to every page or just a range.

    Cropping is not redaction

    The area outside the crop box is hidden, not deleted — it is still in the file and can be revealed with editing software. If you need to remove confidential content, delete the pages with Remove Pages or recreate the document without it. To shrink the file itself, use Compress PDF.

    Crop PDF — FAQ

    How do I crop a PDF for free?

    Drop it in above, set the margins or detect them automatically, check the preview, and download. Nothing is uploaded.

    Does cropping reduce quality?

    No. Pages aren't re-rendered, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution.

    Is the cropped-away content deleted?

    No — it's hidden, not removed, and can be revealed with editing software. Don't use cropping to hide confidential information.

    Can I crop only some pages?

    Yes. Enter a range like 1-3, 5, or leave it empty to crop every page.
