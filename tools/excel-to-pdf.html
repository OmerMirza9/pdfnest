    Convert Excel to PDF without uploading your data

    Turning a spreadsheet into a PDF makes it easy to share — the recipient sees a fixed, tidy table that opens on any device and can't be accidentally changed. PDFNest reads your .xlsx, .xls or .csv file entirely in your browser, rebuilds each worksheet as a clean table, and hands it to your browser's built-in PDF engine. Because nothing is uploaded, even sensitive financial data, invoices or contact lists never leave your computer.

    What gets converted

    Every worksheet's cell values and table structure are converted faithfully, each sheet under its own heading. Charts, conditional formatting and elaborate cell styling are not carried over — the tables are rebuilt from the data for a clean, readable result. For very wide spreadsheets, choose landscape so more columns fit across the page.

    Excel to PDF — FAQ

    How do I convert an Excel file to PDF for free?

    Drop your .xlsx, .xls or .csv in above, pick portrait or landscape, and click Convert to PDF. PDFNest builds the tables in your browser and opens a print dialog — choose “Save as PDF”.

    Are all my sheets converted?

    Yes — every worksheet is included, each under its own heading.

    Does it upload my spreadsheet?

    No — the file is read and rendered entirely in your browser, so your data never leaves your device.

    Will charts and cell colors be kept?

    Cell values and table structure convert faithfully. Charts and complex styling aren't carried over, for a clean, readable layout.

    Do I need Microsoft Excel?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser — no Office, no account, no Adobe.
