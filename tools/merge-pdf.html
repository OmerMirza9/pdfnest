    Combine PDFs the private way

    Merging PDFs is one of the most common document tasks — joining several invoices into one file, combining scanned pages, or assembling a report from multiple sources. PDFNest's merge tool does this entirely inside your browser, so your documents are never uploaded anywhere. The moment you close the tab, nothing remains.

    You can reorder files with a simple drag, remove any you added by mistake, and combine an unlimited number of PDFs. The result keeps the original quality of every page.

    Merge PDF — FAQ

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    As many as you like. Because the work happens on your device, the only limit is your device's available memory.

    Will merging reduce the quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text stays sharp and images keep their original resolution.

    Can I change the page order?

    Yes. Drag any file up or down in the list before merging to control where its pages appear.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your files never reach our servers.
