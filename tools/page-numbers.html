    Professional page numbers in seconds

    Page numbers make long documents easy to reference and look more polished — essential for contracts, reports, manuscripts and study notes. PDFNest lets you add them in the position and format that suits your document, including counters like “Page 3 of 24”.

    You can even change the starting number, which is handy when your PDF is one section of a larger document. The numbers are drawn directly onto the pages and your file is processed entirely in your browser.

    Page Numbers — FAQ

    Can I start from a number other than 1?

    Yes. Set the “Start at” value to any number you like.

    Where can the numbers go?

    Any of six positions: bottom or top, and left, center or right.

    Can I show the total page count?

    Yes. Choose the “Page 1 of N” or “1 / N” format.
