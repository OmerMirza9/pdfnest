    Get the words out of any PDF

    Need to reuse the wording from a report, quote a contract, or move content into another document? PDFNest's PDF‑to‑Text tool extracts the selectable text from your PDF and hands it back as plain text you can paste anywhere. It works entirely in your browser, so even sensitive documents stay private.

    Note that scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) contain no real text, so they need OCR rather than simple extraction — this tool works best on PDFs created from digital documents.

    PDF to Text — FAQ

    Does it keep the formatting?

    It extracts the text content as plain text. Fonts, colours and exact layout are not preserved — it's designed for reusing the words.

    Will it work on a scanned PDF?

    Scanned pages are images without real text, so extraction returns little or nothing. Those need OCR.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. The text is extracted locally in your browser; your file never leaves your device.
