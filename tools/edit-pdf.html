    Mark up and sign PDFs in your browser

    Need to fill a gap, add a note, highlight a clause, or sign on the dotted line? Edit PDF lets you place text, freehand drawings, highlights and images anywhere on the page, then saves a new PDF — all without uploading the file. It's ideal for quick fills, comments, reviews and signatures.

    One honest note: this adds new content on top of the pages. Re-typing a PDF's original embedded text in place is a heavyweight desktop feature; for everyday markup, annotation is what most people actually need. To rearrange pages instead, use Organize PDF; to stamp every page, use Watermark PDF.

    Edit PDF — FAQ

    How do I edit a PDF for free?

    Drop your PDF in above, use the text/draw/highlight/image tools, then click Save. Nothing is uploaded.

    Can I add a signature?

    Yes — use the Image tool to drop in a photo or PNG of your signature and place it where you sign.

    Can I edit the existing text in place?

    This tool adds new content on top of the pages. Editing the original embedded text needs desktop software like Acrobat Pro.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No — editing happens entirely in your browser.
