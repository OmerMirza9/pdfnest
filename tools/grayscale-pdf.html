    Grayscale or black and white?

    Grayscale replaces every colour with a matching shade of grey. Photos, charts and diagrams stay readable, and the document looks the way it would on a mono printer. Choose it for reports, slides and anything with images.

    Black and white goes further and makes every pixel either pure black or pure white. Faint pencil, grey scanner backgrounds and yellowed paper disappear, leaving crisp, high-contrast text — ideal for scanned forms and receipts, and often a smaller file. Photos lose their detail in this mode.

    What to expect

    To remove colour reliably from every kind of content, each page is rebuilt as an image. The result looks right everywhere, but text is no longer selectable or searchable — run it through OCR PDF if you need that back. The conversion happens entirely in your browser; for a smaller file afterwards, try Compress PDF.
