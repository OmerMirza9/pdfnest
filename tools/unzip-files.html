Open ZIP archives anywhere — without uploading them

Sometimes you just need one document out of an archive someone sent — on a phone, a tablet, or a machine where you can't install anything. PDFNest reads the ZIP entirely in your browser: the archive is parsed on your device, you see everything inside, and only the files you choose are extracted. Nothing is uploaded, so a ZIP full of contracts is as safe here as on your own disk.

Two honest limits: password-protected archives can't be opened in the browser (use your computer's built-in tool and the password), and exotic formats like RAR or 7z aren't supported — this tool is for standard .zip files, which is what nearly everything sends. Need to go the other way? Create a ZIP from files or a whole folder.

Unzip — FAQ

How do I open a ZIP online?

Drop the .zip in above — every file inside is listed, each with a download button. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I extract just one file?

Yes — download any single file without extracting the rest.

Do password-protected ZIPs work?

No — encrypted archives can't be opened in the browser. Open them with your computer's archive tool and the password.

Is my archive private?

Yes — it's read and extracted locally and never uploaded.
